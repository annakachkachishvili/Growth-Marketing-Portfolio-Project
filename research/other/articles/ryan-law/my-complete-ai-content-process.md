# My Complete AI Content Process for Ahrefs

- **Author:** Ryan Law (Director of Content Marketing, Ahrefs)
- **Source:** https://ahrefs.com/blog/my-complete-ai-content-process-for-ahrefs/
- **Published:** 5 August 2025
- **Captured:** 2026-06-12 — *structured notes + key passages, not a full copy of the article.*

## Why it's here
This is the *predecessor* to his Claude Code system (above) — the ChatGPT-Projects version. It's the clearest articulation of the **principles** behind AI content that performs, which carry over to any tool.

## Core thesis
Use AI to compress the "hygiene" tasks (the 500th content-gap article, listicles, updates) from days into a couple of hours — and reinvest that time in original research and opinion. His AI-generated articles "perform as well as human-written content," sometimes better.

## The process (7 steps)
1. **Document your existing writing process as Markdown files** — break the editorial process into specific, manageable steps (topic selection → briefing → outlining → structural editing → drafting → product mentions → line editing → internal links → metadata → shortcodes). Add favourite writing-advice posts, course excerpts, and real human-written samples to emulate.
2. **Create a ChatGPT Project** and upload those process docs; set custom instructions that apply to every chat.
3. **Deep research** for topics that need fresh external facts; feed an AI summary into the brief.
4. **Front-load a simple content brief** — target keyword, working title (for intent), key points/anecdotes to include, subtopics (from Ahrefs **AI Content Helper** SERP analysis), and specific Ahrefs products to mention.
5. **Read, review, nudge** — act as editor through outline → draft (in ChatGPT Canvas), leaving the same inline comments he'd give a human: "be specific, cut weasel words," "add a real example," "correct this," "trim/expand," "make it beginner-friendly."
6. **Generate metadata, WordPress shortcodes, and internal links** (notes the LLM hallucinates link URLs — plans to feed it a real URL list; Site Audit catches broken links). Images are still added manually.
7. **Monitor performance** — tracks AI articles in Ahrefs Portfolios + Web Analytics.

## Key passages (quoted, attributed)
> "90% of success comes from good topic selection… I only use AI for topics where I have some passing knowledge of the subject matter (so that I can tell if the article is actually good)."

> "Using AI is no excuse for publishing shoddy content."

## Data point
Ahrefs found a **correlation of 0.011 (≈ zero)** between AI-content use and ranking position — i.e. Google doesn't penalize AI content per se, as long as it isn't mass-produced spam.

## "Pareto prompts" worth stealing (his most-effective instructions)
- Always consult the project documentation and work in sequential order.
- Include a fixed summary of the target audience.
- Tell the model to roleplay as someone with "in-the-trenches experience" (drives first-person anecdotes/examples).

## Playbook takeaway
Even before Claude Code: **process docs + a front-loaded brief + AI Content Helper subtopics + human review against a clear bar.** Topic selection and human judgment are the 90%; the tool is the 10%.
