## Why it's here
The most **comprehensive, free, structured curriculum** for AI search optimization — an ideal scaffold for the *table of contents* of a real playbook.

## Core framing
In AI search, the goal **shifts from "ranking well" to becoming citable, semantically clear, and contextually trusted.** Optimization is organized as a roadmap with stages/areas rather than a single tactic.

## The roadmap areas (LearningAISearch)
Free, non-sponsored guides covering, by stage:
1. **Fundamentals** — how AI search (GEO/AEO/LLMO) actually works.
2. **Technical configuration** — crawlability/accessibility for AI bots, structured data, the emerging agent-readiness files.
3. **Content optimization** — the core (see below).
4. **Brand authority & citations** — being mentioned/recommended across trusted third-party sources.
5. **Measurement** — tracking AI visibility (mentions, citations, share of voice).
6. **Tools** — the current AI-search optimization & tracking tools.

## Content optimization specifics (the part most relevant to a content playbook)
- **Topical clusters:** a comprehensive **pillar page** for broad topics + **cluster pages** around specific facets — covering the topic with breadth *and* depth so you answer the full **query fan-out**.
- **Chunking / passage optimization:** structure content into self-contained, retrievable passages (clear question → direct answer) so AI systems can lift the right chunk.
- **Focus on meaningful business topics** where you can provide comprehensive, useful, expert answers — not thin coverage of everything.

## Playbook takeaway
Use Aleyda's roadmap as the **skeleton** of the playbook (fundamentals → technical → content → authority → measurement → tools), and her content rules — pillar/cluster topical coverage + passage-level chunking for fan-out queries — as the on-page standard. Pairs directly with Kevin Indig's "front-load + entity-dense passages" citation data.
