# The AI Slop Loop

- **Author:** Lily Ray (VP SEO & AI Search, Amsive; founder, Algorythmic)
- **Source:** https://lilyraynyc.substack.com/p/the-ai-slop-loop
- **Published:** April 2026
- **Captured:** 2026-06-12 — *structured notes + key passages, not a full copy of the article.*

## Core thesis
AI search systems treat **repetition as consensus**: if enough sources repeat a claim, RAG-based systems (Perplexity, AI Overviews, ChatGPT) present it as fact — whether or not a human ever verified it. AI slop seeds a **misinformation feedback loop** that then becomes retrieval/training data for the next round of AI answers.

## The evidence (what makes this strong)
- **The fake "September 2025 Perspectives update":** Perplexity described a Google core update that never happened, citing two AI-generated agency posts. Months later, LLMs still confidently describe it.
- **The pizza experiment:** she published an AI-generated post about a fake update, including that Google "approved the update between slices of leftover pizza." **Within 24 hours, AI Overviews served the fabricated detail back as fact.** (She deleted the post because it was spreading.)
- **The BBC "hot dog" test:** a fake "best tech journalists at eating hot dogs" page was parroted by Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT within 24 hours (Claude did not).
- **NYT data:** AI Overviews were accurate ~91% of the time — but **56% of *correct* answers were "ungrounded"** (the cited sources didn't fully support the claim), and that got *worse* with the newer model (37% → 56%).
- **Tiered accuracy:** ~94% of ChatGPT users are on the free tier; the more accurate models are paywalled, so most users get the least reliable answers — and the systems "never admit uncertainty."

## Key passage (quoted, attributed)
> "Until that changes, the burden of fact-checking falls on the user. And most users don't know they're carrying it."

## Playbook takeaway
1. **Quality/verification is a moat.** Mandate human fact-checking and primary-source grounding — to avoid publishing slop, and because accurate, well-sourced content is what survives as AI systems clean up.
2. **Don't learn SEO/GEO tactics *from* an LLM** — the space is contaminated with fabricated advice. Verify against named, experienced experts. (Exactly why this project collected from real practitioners and verified every link.)
